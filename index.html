Shopping international brands is hard work

Shoppers in Georgia buy from US and European stores every day. Inboxes fill with “up to 70% off” newsletters, prices change all the time, and it is easy to pay for a “sale” that is no sale at all, or to order the wrong size from a brand you have never tried on.

How Smart Stylist helps

1

Tracks the sales you care about

Reads brand sale newsletters and saved products, and keeps only the items that match your style, wardrobe plan and budget.

2

Checks if a discount is real

Compares today’s price with the price history we have recorded, so you can see whether “40% off” is a true low or an inflated original price.

3

Helps you choose the right size

Uses each brand’s size chart and your own fit notes from past orders to suggest the size most likely to fit.

4

Shows the full cost

Estimates the delivered cost to Georgia, including shipping, so the price you compare is the price you pay.

Chrome extension

Save any product while you browse

With the Smart Stylist extension, one click on a product page adds it to your watch list. We record its price over time and let you know when it drops to a price worth buying, together with a short, clear verdict.

When you choose to buy, we send you to the brand’s own official store. You always buy directly from the retailer.

About

Smart Stylist is an independent project built in Tbilisi, Georgia by a software developer who shops international brands and found the process slow and confusing. We started with the shopper’s biggest problem, knowing what to buy and when, and are building the tools step by step with early users.

The service is currently in early access for shoppers in Georgia, with Armenia planned next.
