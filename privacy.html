Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what information Smart Stylist (“we”, “us”) collects through this website, our app and our Chrome extension, and how we use it. Smart Stylist is operated by an individual developer based in Tbilisi, Georgia.

Information we collect

  • Account information you give us, such as your email address and name.
  • Style and fit preferences you choose to share, such as sizes, favourite brands, budget and notes on how past items fitted.
  • Products you save with the extension: the page address, product name, price, currency and image of the product you clicked to save. The extension only reads a page when you click it; it does not track your general browsing.
  • Sale newsletters, if you choose to forward them to us or connect a mailbox, so we can extract product prices. We only process messages from retailers.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and error logs, used to keep the service working.

This website itself does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

How we use it

  • To show your watch list, price history, size suggestions and buying verdicts.
  • To send you alerts you asked for, such as a price drop.
  • To improve the accuracy of price history and size suggestions.

We do not sell your personal information.

Affiliate links

When you click a link to a retailer, it may be an affiliate link through a network such as Awin. The network and the retailer may use cookies to record that the visit came from Smart Stylist, so we can receive a commission if you make a purchase. Their use of that data is covered by their own privacy policies. See our affiliate disclosure.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers that help us run Smart Stylist, such as hosting, email delivery and AI processing providers, and only as needed to provide the service, or when required by law.

Storage and retention

We keep your data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time, and we will do so within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and you can withdraw any consent you gave. Under the Law of Georgia on Personal Data Protection, and the GDPR where it applies, you may also complain to the relevant data protection authority.

Children

Smart Stylist is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell registered users.
